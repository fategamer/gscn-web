Intelligence standard

From signal to decision.

Every GSCN research product should move through a disciplined chain: understand the evidence, interpret the implications, then define what action can follow.

01
Signal

Identify the market, operational or policy development that matters.

02
Evidence

Separate verifiable information from assumptions, noise and unsupported claims.

03
Analysis

Translate evidence into implications for procurement and supply networks.

04
Action

Turn insight into practical decisions, metrics or next steps.

Research principle: GSCN does not present speculation as evidence. Where data is incomplete, the limitation should be visible.
