Decision framework

Evidence before intervention.

A market movement can be a signal without being proof. GSCN separates observation from assessment and assessment from action.

01 · SignalWhat changed in the market, category or operating environment?
02 · ExposureWhich category, supplier, contract, route or dependency is affected?
03 · EvidenceWhat verified data supports the assessment, and what remains unknown?
04 · ActionWhat sourcing, mitigation, governance or monitoring response is justified?
What GSCN watches

Signals that deserve procurement attention.

MarketInflation, GDP, trade intensity and macro pressure.
SupplierConcentration, dependency, continuity and evidence quality.
FlowLead times, routes, ports, inventory and logistics activity.
GovernanceCompetition, controls, documentation and accountability.

The current public data layer is an analytical foundation, not a substitute for contract-level, supplier-level or category-level due diligence. Screening indicators are prompts for investigation, not formal ratings or forecasts.
