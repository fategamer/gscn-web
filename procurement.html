GSCN · Procurement Intelligence

From procurement activity to strategic intelligence.

GSCN connects market signals, category exposure, supplier evidence and governance into a disciplined framework for better sourcing decisions.

Insight → Strategy → Execution

The intelligence standard: identify the signal, establish the evidence, assess exposure, then translate it into an actionable procurement response.

Core intelligence

Four dimensions of procurement exposure.

01 · MARKET

Cost & market pressure

Inflation, trade conditions, logistics activity and other market signals that can change category economics.

02 · CATEGORY

Category exposure

Criticality, spend concentration, dependency, substitution options and cost-driver sensitivity.

03 · SUPPLIER

Supplier intelligence

Continuity, concentration, performance evidence, dependency and supplier-specific exposure.

04 · GOVERNANCE

Integrity & control

Transparency, competition, documentation, accountability and responsible procurement controls.

05 · RESILIENCE

Continuity planning

Lead-time exposure, alternate sourcing, route dependency, inventory protection and disruption response.

06 · ANALYTICS

Decision intelligence

Turn verified observations into monitoring signals, risk screens and decision-ready procurement insight.

Decision framework

Evidence before intervention.

GSCN does not treat a single macro indicator as proof of supplier or procurement risk. Exposure must be established through multiple evidence layers.

SignalWhat changed in the market or operating environment?
ExposureWhich category, supplier, contract or route is affected?
EvidenceWhat verified data supports the assessment?
ActionWhat sourcing, mitigation or monitoring response is justified?
