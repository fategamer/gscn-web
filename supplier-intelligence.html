GSCN · Supplier Intelligence

Know the supplier exposure before it becomes disruption.

A structured intelligence layer for understanding supplier dependency, verification, continuity and performance evidence. GSCN does not publish invented supplier scores or unverified claims.

Supplier intelligence is evidence, not a reputation list.

A credible supplier assessment requires identifiable evidence, a defined period, a clear methodology and appropriate verification. Public market indicators can signal exposure; they cannot by themselves prove supplier failure or misconduct.

Supplier risk architecture

Six dimensions of supplier exposure.

01 · IDENTITY

Verification

Legal identity, organisation details, registration evidence and verification status.

02 · DEPENDENCY

Concentration

Share of spend, category dependency, single-source exposure and substitution availability.

03 · CONTINUITY

Resilience

Lead-time exposure, geographic dependency, alternate supply and disruption preparedness.

04 · PERFORMANCE

Delivery evidence

Documented quality, delivery, service-level and contract-performance observations.

05 · FINANCIAL

Financial exposure

Where legally and reliably available: financial health indicators, payment exposure and dependency.

06 · GOVERNANCE

Integrity controls

Conflict controls, competition, documentation, compliance and responsible procurement evidence.

Intelligence workflow

Build the supplier picture layer by layer.

1 · VerifyEstablish who the supplier is and what evidence is available.
2 · QuantifyMeasure dependency, spend concentration, criticality and exposure.
3 · MonitorTrack performance, market signals, continuity and material changes.
4 · ActMitigate, diversify, negotiate, monitor or escalate based on evidence.
