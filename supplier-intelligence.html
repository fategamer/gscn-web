GSCN · Supplier Intelligence

Know the supplier exposure before it becomes disruption.

A structured intelligence layer for supplier dependency, verification, continuity and performance evidence. GSCN does not publish invented supplier scores or unverified claims.

Supplier intelligence is evidence, not a reputation list.

A credible supplier assessment requires identifiable evidence, a defined period, a clear methodology and appropriate verification.

Supplier risk architecture

Six dimensions of supplier exposure.

01 · IDENTITY

Verification

Legal identity, organisation details, registration evidence and verification status.

02 · DEPENDENCY

Concentration

Share of spend, category dependency, single-source exposure and substitution availability.

03 · CONTINUITY

Resilience

Lead-time exposure, geographic dependency, alternate supply and disruption preparedness.

04 · PERFORMANCE

Delivery evidence

Documented quality, delivery, service-level and contract-performance observations.

05 · FINANCIAL

Financial exposure

Where legally and reliably available: financial health indicators and dependency evidence.

06 · GOVERNANCE

Integrity controls

Competition, documentation, compliance and responsible procurement evidence.

Intelligence workflow

Build the supplier picture layer by layer.

1 · VerifyEstablish identity and evidence.
2 · QuantifyMeasure dependency and criticality.
3 · MonitorTrack performance and material changes.
4 · ActMitigate, diversify, negotiate or escalate.
