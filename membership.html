Membership pathways

One network. Different stages.

GSCN is structured to support professionals at different points in their careers without compromising the same standard of evidence, integrity and contribution.

01ProfessionalFor practitioners and specialists working across procurement, supply chain, logistics and related functions.→
02Emerging TalentFor students and early-career professionals building practical capability and professional networks.→
03Research & ContributorFor researchers, writers, analysts and subject-matter contributors advancing useful knowledge.→
04InstitutionalFor organisations seeking structured collaboration, knowledge exchange and professional engagement.→

How it works

Start simple. Build properly.

The current membership entry point is an application. GSCN can review submissions before adding eligible professionals to the public directory or future member systems.

Step 01

Apply

Send your professional details and the pathway that best describes you.

Step 02

Review

GSCN reviews the submission for completeness and appropriate directory placement.

Step 03

Connect

Approved profiles can enter the directory while deeper member infrastructure is developed.
